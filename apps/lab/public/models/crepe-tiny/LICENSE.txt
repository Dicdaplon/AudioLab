crepe-tiny.bin: the "tiny" CREPE pitch-estimation model, weights converted to
float16 by packages/core/scripts/convertCrepeModel.ts from the TF.js export
published at https://github.com/marl/crepe (gh-pages branch, model/).
No weight was retrained or altered beyond the float16 rounding.

Kim, J. W., Salamon, J., Li, P. & Bello, J. P. (2018). CREPE: A Convolutional
Representation for Pitch Estimation. Proc. IEEE ICASSP, 161-165.
