`HowPanel`, a discreet "How?" disclosure for explaining a feature (diagram, idea, algorithm,
limits, sources, one thing worth knowing), and the `Sketch*` miniature diagrams (`SketchChain`,
`SketchWave`, `SketchBars`, `SketchCurve`, `SketchTimeline`, `SketchMeter`, `SketchLabel`), themed by
the Loupe tokens. `styles.css` adds the `.al-how` and `.al-sketch` classes. Every feature of the lab
now has its sheet; the recipe is in docs/HOW-SHEETS.md.
